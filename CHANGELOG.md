# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

**Dragon's Lair comes to the Atari ST.** The 1983 laserdisc game, Don Bluth's cartoon and all, on
an ST, STE, Mega ST or Mega STE with a SidecarTridge Multi-device: Dirk the Daring, Princess
Daphne and Singe the dragon, by the arcade's own rules.

- **The whole game**, full screen at 25 pictures a second in 16 colours per picture, with its
  sound, on the joystick or the keyboard.
- **A start menu** in place of "insert coins": oldies mode (the move to make drawn over the
  picture), infinite lives or 1 to 5, the arcade's scene order or a fixed one, watch mode,
  continue, input sounds, relaxed or arcade timing, retry after a death, and start at any scene.
- **Pause, the volume and ten high scores**, all remembered; the arcade's up-and-left secret too.
- **Your own CD-ROM**: Dragon's Lair CD-ROM (Version 3.1) by Digital Leisure, the only edition
  that works. If your disc no longer reads, the Internet Archive keeps a copy for preservation:
  look for "Dragon's Lair CD-ROM (Version 3.1)" on archive.org.
- **The clips made in minutes** by the web converter at <https://md-dragons-lair.sidecartridge.com>,
  in your browser, or in about an hour by the cartridge itself from the image on the card.

The [user guide](https://github.com/sidecartridge/md-dragons-lair/blob/main/docs/guide.md) has a
picture of every screen. Thanks to [DirkSimple](https://github.com/icculus/DirkSimple), the
[SNES Super Dragon's Lair Arcade](https://github.com/astrobleem/SNES-SuperDragonsLairArcade)
project, [pl_mpeg](https://github.com/phoboslab/pl_mpeg) and Nayuki's QR Code generator.
